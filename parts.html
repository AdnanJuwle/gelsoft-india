Gel, soaked properly

Size

7–8 mm is the standard for everything we sell. Undersized gel double-feeds and jams; oversized gel will not chamber at all.

Soak time

Three to four hours in clean water, then drain. Left overnight they swell soft and mush inside the hop-up, which reads as a dying gearbox and is not one.

Hardness

Competition gel is harder and rounder, so it chronos consistently and flies straighter. It also stings more, which is why some hosts cap it.
