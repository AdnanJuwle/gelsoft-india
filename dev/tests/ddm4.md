# TG DDM4 PDW Electric Gel Blaster – Tan

The **TG DDM4 PDW** is an electric-powered gel blaster featuring a modern PDW-inspired design, high-strength nylon construction and metal internal gearing. Finished in tan, it features a retractable stock, ergonomic grip, detachable metal magazine and front and rear sights.

Powered by a rechargeable 7.4V lithium battery, it offers single-shot and continuous-fire modes, with a manufacturer-rated firing speed of approximately 15 rounds per second. Its compact design measures approximately 63 cm in overall length.

### Key Features
- Electric-powered operation with rechargeable 7.4V battery
- Single-shot and continuous-fire modes
- Approximately 15 RPS (manufacturer-rated)
- Metal internal gear system
- High-strength nylon construction
- Metal-powered magazine
- Retractable / adjustable stock
- Front and rear sights
- Accessory mounting rail
