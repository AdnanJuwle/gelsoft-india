Dressing for Indian heat

A full plate carrier over a long-sleeve combat shirt is a two-hour game at best in April. For most of the year a chest rig over a breathable tee carries the same four mags with half the trapped heat, and anti-fog matters far more here than lens tint — a fogged goggle is the single most common reason people lift eye protection mid-game, which is exactly how injuries happen.
